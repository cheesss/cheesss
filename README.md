# Hyeonjun Cho

Undergraduate researcher in robot learning at Sungkyunkwan University.

I study learning-based manipulation under distribution shift, with an emphasis on imitation learning, vision-language planning, safety interventions, and recovery on real robot systems.

[Research portfolio](https://cheesss.github.io/) · [Academic CV](https://cheesss.github.io/cv/Hyeonjun_Cho_Academic_CV_20260805.pdf) · [Email](mailto:chohjender@g.skku.edu)

## Selected research

### Recovery-Aware LPB for RMP-Induced OOD States

Designed a recovery-aware policy bridge and evaluated it in 160 RB10 trials. The proposed method completed 146/160 trials (91.3%) in the reported intervention setting.

[Project case study](https://cheesss.github.io/research/recovery-aware-lpb/) · [Public research code](https://github.com/cheesss/recovery-aware-lpb-rb10)

### Grounded Language-to-Motion Planning with Memory and Gripper Constraints

Integrated visual grounding, execution memory, and gripper-aware grasp selection. Success improved from 9/15 to 12/15 in the defined evaluation.

[Project case study](https://cheesss.github.io/research/vlm-lmtg/) · [Research fork](https://github.com/cheesss/folk-language-models-trajectory-generators/tree/VLM_memory_LMTG_realWorld)

### LLM-Based Zero-Shot Trajectory Generation

Implemented structured waypoint generation, collision-aware validation, simulation experiments, and early RB10 integration.

[Project case study](https://cheesss.github.io/research/llm-trajectory-generation/) · [Research fork](https://github.com/cheesss/language-models-trajectory-generators)
